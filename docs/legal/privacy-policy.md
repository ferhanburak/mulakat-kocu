## 2. What we process

| Data | When | Where it is kept |
|---|---|---|
| Name, email address | When you create an account | On our server (Supabase) |
| Password | When you create an account | On our server, only as a one-way hash; we cannot see your password |
| Target profession, field, career stage, daily goal | During profile setup | On our server if you have an account, otherwise only on your device |
| Your interview answers, scores, reports, XP, streak, badges, mistake notebook | As you use the App | On our server if you have an account, otherwise only on your device |
| App language, theme, reminder time, spoken-question preference | When you change settings | Only on your device |

**What we don't process:** We don't access your location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or advertising ID. The App has no ads, analytics or tracking tools. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your device; no data is sent to a server for them.

**Guest mode:** If you use the App without an account, your data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

## 3. Why we process it (purpose and legal basis)

- **To create your account, let you sign in and reset your password:** performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
- **To store and sync your progress, evaluate your answers and create reports:** performance of a contract (KVKK art. 5/2-c; GDPR art. 6(1)(b)).
- **Security, preventing abuse and legal obligations:** legitimate interests and legal obligation (KVKK art. 5/2-ç, f; GDPR art. 6(1)(c), (f)).

Your answers are currently evaluated by a rule-based system on your device; they are not sent to any third-party AI service. If this changes, we will update this policy and tell you in advance.

## 4. Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or share it for advertising. It is only transferred to the infrastructure provider we use to run the service:

- **Supabase Inc.:** authentication and database. Data is stored on servers in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany).

Because this provider is outside Turkey, the transfer under KVKK art. 9 is based on [your explicit consent / standard contractual clauses notified to the Authority]. *(Clause to be reviewed by a lawyer.)*

We may share data with competent authorities where the law requires it.

## 5. How long we keep it

As long as your account exists. When you delete your account, your name, email and progress are permanently deleted from our server, except where the law requires us to keep something.

## 6. Deleting your account and data

You can permanently delete your account and all its data on our server in the App under **Profile → Account → Delete my account**. If you can't access the App, email [contact email] from the address registered on your account.

## 7. Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11 and the GDPR you can:

- find out whether we process your data and request information about it
- learn the purpose of processing and whether data is used for that purpose
- know the third parties it is transferred to
- ask for incomplete or incorrect data to be corrected
- ask for your data to be deleted
- object to an outcome against you that results solely from automated analysis
- claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing

Send requests to [contact email]. We reply within 30 days at the latest.

## 8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). On the server, each user can only access their own data (row-level security). Passwords are stored as one-way hashes.

## 9. Children

The App is not intended for people under 16. We delete accounts we learn belong to someone under 16.
